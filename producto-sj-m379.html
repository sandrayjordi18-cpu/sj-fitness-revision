Measurement guide · S&J Precision Fit

Two measurements,
one piece.

Waist measurement

How to measure your waist

  1. 01

    Get ready

    Use a flexible sewing tape measure that does not stretch, with clearly marked centimetres. Do not use a metal tape or a warped tape. Measure directly on the skin, standing, with your body relaxed and without tensing your abdomen.

  2. 02

    Place the tape

    Wrap the tape around your body three fingers below your navel. Check that it is level all the way round, including at the back, with no twists or folds.

  3. 03

    Snug, not tight

    The tape should touch the skin without sinking in or hanging loose.

  4. 04

    Read and check

    Breathe normally and take the measurement at the end of a breath out, without forcing it. Repeat the measurement and note the waist in centimetres, without adding or subtracting anything.

Trunk rise

How to measure the trunk rise

  1. 01

    Place the start

    Place the start of the tape at the centre of your back, slightly above the tailbone.

  2. 02

    Follow the path

    Bring the tape down and pass it between your legs, following the contour of your body.

  3. 03

    Mark the end

    Stop just before the testicles. Keep the tape in contact with your body, without pressing or pulling it tight.

  4. 04

    Read and check

    Note the measurement in centimetres and repeat it to check. Do not add or subtract centimetres. If it is hard to keep the start in place, ask someone to help.

Important: this is the path our atelier uses. Do not replace it with a measurement from front waist to back waist.
