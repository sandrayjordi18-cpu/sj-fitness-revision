About Bodybuilding posing trunks

A Bodybuilding posing trunk has one job on stage: frame the physique without distracting from it. Rise, side width and leg line change how the legs and waist read from the judges’ table, which is why we draft every pattern from your own measurements instead of a size chart.

Choose a design, add your competition date and the atelier will guide your measurements after you order. Provisional SEO text
